## Introduction

This customer complaint form template is a general intake form for water districts, small offices, LGUs, barangays, and local service providers that need a structured way to receive, record, route, and monitor service complaints.

The form should collect enough information to understand and act on the concern, while avoiding unnecessary sensitive personal information. Offices should handle all submitted information according to applicable privacy, records, and data protection rules.

## When to Use This Resource

Use this form for complaints or service concerns involving:

- Water service interruption, low pressure, or water quality concerns
- Billing, account, or payment concerns
- Delayed service request processing
- Office service experience or staff coordination concerns
- Facility, maintenance, or public-service issues
- Follow-up requests that need tracking and acknowledgment

## Notes for Customization

- Collect only information needed to act on the complaint.
- Avoid collecting sensitive personal information unless clearly required and authorized.
- Include a tracking number if the office monitors complaint status.
- Identify who receives, routes, acts on, and closes complaints.
- Add target response times only if the office has approved service standards.
- Keep complaint records in a secure location with controlled access.
- For digital forms, include an appropriate privacy notice before submission.

## Disclaimer

This template is for general customer service and records assistance only. Offices should adapt it according to their approved complaint-handling procedure, data privacy policy, records retention rules, and service standards.

The form should not be used to collect unnecessary sensitive information, and submitted information should be handled only for legitimate office purposes.
