## Introduction

This barangay resolution template is a general drafting guide for barangay councils, committees, and local offices that need to prepare a formal resolution for a project, activity, policy, request, endorsement, or other official action.

Barangay resolutions may be subject to specific legal, procedural, voting, publication, posting, approval, or recordkeeping requirements. This template is intended to help organize the draft language, but the final document should be reviewed by the proper barangay officials and adapted to the applicable local process.

## When to Use This Resource

Use this resource when preparing an initial draft of a barangay resolution for matters such as:

- Supporting or endorsing a local project
- Authorizing a barangay activity or program
- Requesting assistance, equipment, funding, or coordination
- Adopting a committee recommendation
- Approving a local administrative action
- Recording the official position of the barangay council
- Supporting documents for a project proposal or request letter

## Template or Guide Body

```text
Republic of the Philippines
Province of [Province]
City/Municipality of [City/Municipality]
Barangay [Barangay Name]

OFFICE OF THE SANGGUNIANG BARANGAY

BARANGAY RESOLUTION NO. [Resolution Number]
Series of [Year]

A RESOLUTION [STATE THE MAIN PURPOSE OF THE RESOLUTION IN ALL CAPS]

WHEREAS, [state the background, need, policy basis, project context, request, or concern being addressed];

WHEREAS, [state the relevant facts, community benefit, office requirement, or reason for the proposed action];

WHEREAS, [state any meeting, consultation, committee recommendation, request, proposal, report, or supporting document considered by the barangay council];

WHEREAS, the Sangguniang Barangay recognizes the need to [briefly state the action or objective];

NOW THEREFORE, on motion of Barangay Kagawad [Name], duly seconded by Barangay Kagawad [Name], be it:

RESOLVED, as it is hereby resolved, that the Sangguniang Barangay of [Barangay Name], [City/Municipality], [Province], hereby [state the specific action being approved, endorsed, requested, supported, authorized, or adopted];

RESOLVED FURTHER, that [state any supporting action, such as authorizing the Punong Barangay to sign documents, transmit the resolution, coordinate with another office, or submit requirements];

RESOLVED FINALLY, that copies of this resolution be furnished to [name of office, agency, department, organization, or person] for information, appropriate action, and/or record purposes.

ADOPTED this [day] day of [month], [year], during the regular/special session of the Sangguniang Barangay held at [venue], Barangay [Barangay Name], [City/Municipality], [Province].

Certified correct:

[Name of Barangay Secretary]
Barangay Secretary

Attested by:

[Name of Punong Barangay]
Punong Barangay / Presiding Officer

Approved by:

[Name of Punong Barangay, if applicable]
Punong Barangay

Members present:

1. [Name], Barangay Kagawad
2. [Name], Barangay Kagawad
3. [Name], Barangay Kagawad
4. [Name], Barangay Kagawad
5. [Name], Barangay Kagawad
6. [Name], Barangay Kagawad
7. [Name], Barangay Kagawad

Absent:

[Name/s, if any]
```

## Notes for Customization

- Replace all bracketed placeholders with the correct barangay, date, official names, and resolution details.
- Confirm the proper resolution numbering format used by the barangay.
- Adjust the "WHEREAS" clauses to explain the factual basis for the resolution clearly and briefly.
- Keep the "RESOLVED" clauses specific. Avoid broad language that is not supported by the meeting record or council action.
- Confirm whether the resolution was adopted during a regular or special session.
- Attach or reference supporting documents when needed, such as project proposals, minutes, request letters, committee reports, or attendance records.
- Review the final format against the barangay's existing resolution format and records procedure.

## Disclaimer

This template is for general reference and drafting assistance only. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee that a resolution is legally sufficient, officially approved, procedurally valid, or compliant with all applicable requirements.

Barangay resolutions should be reviewed and finalized by authorized barangay officials according to applicable laws, local rules, internal procedures, meeting records, voting requirements, and approval processes.
