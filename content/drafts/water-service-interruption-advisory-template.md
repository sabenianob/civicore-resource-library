## Introduction

This water service interruption advisory template is a public communication format for water districts, water service providers, LGUs, or offices that need to inform customers about temporary water service interruptions.

The goal is to provide clear, timely, and practical information about affected areas, schedule, reason for interruption, expected restoration, reminders, and contact channels. Restoration times should be stated carefully and updated when conditions change.

## When to Use This Resource

Use this template when preparing a public advisory for:

- Scheduled maintenance
- Emergency repairs
- Pipe leak repair
- Pump or motor repair
- Power interruption affecting water supply
- Low pressure or no-water events
- Flushing, cleaning, or system improvement works
- Service restoration updates after an interruption

## Template or Guide Body

```text
WATER SERVICE INTERRUPTION ADVISORY

Advisory No.: [Advisory Number, if applicable]
Date Posted: [Date and Time]

The [Name of Water District / Water Service Provider / Office] informs customers of a temporary water service interruption affecting the following areas:

Affected Areas:

- [Barangay / Zone / Sitio / Subdivision / Street / Service Area]
- [Barangay / Zone / Sitio / Subdivision / Street / Service Area]
- [Barangay / Zone / Sitio / Subdivision / Street / Service Area]

Schedule:

Start: [Date and Time]
Estimated Restoration: [Date and Time / Tentative Time / To be announced]

Reason for Interruption:

[Briefly explain the reason, such as scheduled maintenance, emergency repair, pipe leak repair, pump repair, power interruption, or system improvement work.]

Customer Reminders:

- Store enough water for essential needs before the scheduled interruption, if advance notice is possible.
- Keep faucets closed while service is interrupted.
- After service resumes, allow water to run briefly if discoloration or air is observed.
- Customers in elevated or far-end areas may experience delayed restoration or low pressure.
- Please monitor official updates from [Name of Water District / Office].

Contact Information:

For inquiries or updates, please contact:

[Office / Hotline / Contact Number]
[Email Address, if applicable]
[Official Facebook Page / Website, if applicable]

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience and understanding as our team works to restore normal service.

[Name of Water District / Water Service Provider / Office]
```

## Notes for Customization

- Identify affected areas as specifically as possible.
- Use "estimated" or "target" restoration time when the exact time is uncertain.
- Avoid promising exact restoration unless it is confirmed by the operations team.
- For emergency repairs, explain the issue briefly without unnecessary technical detail.
- Update the advisory if the schedule, affected areas, or restoration time changes.
- Include only official contact channels that are actively monitored.
- Keep the public tone clear, calm, and service-oriented.

## Disclaimer

This template is for general communication support only. Water districts and service providers should verify technical details, affected areas, schedules, restoration estimates, and contact information before posting.

Actual restoration may depend on site conditions, repair progress, power availability, pressure recovery, and other operational factors.
