## Introduction

This transmittal letter template is a formal cover letter for sending documents, reports, proposals, forms, attachments, or official submissions to another office, agency, department, barangay, water district, or partner organization.

It helps the sender identify what is being submitted, why it is being transmitted, and what action or acknowledgment is requested from the receiving office.

## When to Use This Resource

Use this template when transmitting:

- Project proposals
- Reports and accomplishment documents
- Request attachments
- Endorsement documents
- Signed forms or certifications
- Meeting minutes or resolutions
- Compliance documents
- Supporting papers for review, approval, filing, or record purposes

## Template or Guide Body

```text
[OFFICE / ORGANIZATION LETTERHEAD]

[Date]

[Name of Recipient]
[Position / Designation]
[Office / Department / Agency]
[Office Address]

Subject: Transmittal of [Document/s Being Submitted]

Dear [Title and Last Name / Sir / Madam]:

Respectfully transmitted herewith are the following documents for your information, review, appropriate action, and/or record purposes:

1. [Document title / description] - [number of copies/pages, if applicable]
2. [Document title / description] - [number of copies/pages, if applicable]
3. [Document title / description] - [number of copies/pages, if applicable]

These documents are submitted in relation to [briefly state the project, request, activity, report, meeting, requirement, or purpose of the transmittal].

We respectfully request your office to acknowledge receipt of the transmitted documents. If further information, clarification, or additional supporting documents are needed, please contact [contact person, position, contact number, and/or email].

Thank you.

Respectfully yours,

[Name of Signatory]
[Position / Designation]
[Office / Organization]
[Contact Number / Email]

Received by:

Name: ______________________________
Position/Office: ____________________
Date and Time Received: _____________
Signature: __________________________
```

## Notes for Customization

- List all transmitted documents clearly and in the same order as the attachments.
- Include copy counts, page counts, or reference numbers when useful.
- State whether the documents are for information, review, approval, appropriate action, or record purposes.
- Keep the tone formal and concise.
- Add a receiving copy or acknowledgment section if the sender needs proof of receipt.
- Use this with the LGU Request Letter Template when documents are being submitted in support of a formal request.

## Disclaimer

This template is for general reference and drafting assistance only. Offices should adapt it according to their document routing, receiving, filing, and signatory procedures.

This template does not guarantee acceptance, approval, compliance, or completeness of the transmitted documents.
