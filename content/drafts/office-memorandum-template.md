## Introduction

This office memorandum template is a practical format for internal office communication. It may be used by LGUs, barangays, water districts, small public-service offices, and administrative teams that need to issue clear instructions, reminders, assignments, meeting notices, policy reminders, or coordination updates.

The template is designed for routine internal communication and should be adjusted to match the office's authority, signatory rules, and document style.

## When to Use This Resource

Use this template when an office needs to communicate internally about:

- Work assignments or staff instructions
- Meeting schedules and attendance reminders
- Submission deadlines
- Office procedures or policy reminders
- Activity coordination
- Document routing or reporting requirements
- Temporary changes in office arrangements
- Follow-up actions after a meeting or directive

## Template or Guide Body

```text
[OFFICE / AGENCY / ORGANIZATION LETTERHEAD]

OFFICE MEMORANDUM

To: [Name/s, Position/s, Unit/s, or All Concerned Personnel]
From: [Name and Position of Issuing Official]
Date: [Date]
Subject: [Clear Subject of the Memorandum]

1. Purpose

This memorandum is issued to [state the purpose, such as provide instructions, remind personnel, announce a schedule, assign responsibilities, or coordinate an activity].

2. Background / Reference

[Briefly explain the reason for the memorandum. Mention any related meeting, office instruction, activity, deadline, report, project, or policy if applicable.]

3. Instructions / Details

All concerned personnel are requested/instructed to observe the following:

a. [Instruction, reminder, or task]
b. [Instruction, reminder, or task]
c. [Instruction, reminder, or task]

4. Action Required

[State the specific action required from the recipients. Include documents to submit, offices to coordinate with, meetings to attend, or tasks to complete.]

5. Deadline / Schedule

The required action should be completed on or before [date and time], unless otherwise advised.

6. Contact / Coordination

For questions or coordination, please contact [name, position, office/unit, contact number, or email].

For strict compliance / For information and guidance / For appropriate action.

[Name of Signatory]
[Position / Designation]
[Office / Unit]
```

## Notes for Customization

- Use a clear subject line that tells the reader exactly what the memorandum is about.
- Keep instructions specific and action-oriented.
- Use "for strict compliance" only when the issuing official has authority and the matter requires mandatory action.
- Use "for information and guidance" for notices, reminders, and general updates.
- Include deadlines only when they are confirmed and realistic.
- Identify the responsible office or person for follow-up questions.
- Keep the memorandum concise when the matter is routine.

## Disclaimer

This template is for general drafting assistance only. Offices should review the final memorandum according to their internal rules, signatory authority, records procedures, and applicable office policies.

This template does not guarantee official approval, legal sufficiency, or compliance with agency-specific requirements.
