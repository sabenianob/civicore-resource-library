## Introduction

This meeting minutes template is a general format for documenting meetings of offices, barangay committees, project teams, technical working groups, local organizations, and small public-service teams.

Good minutes help record attendance, agenda items, discussion summaries, agreements, action items, responsible persons, and deadlines. For official boards, councils, or committees, use the prescribed format and approval procedure when required.

## When to Use This Resource

Use this template for:

- Office coordination meetings
- Barangay committee meetings
- Project implementation meetings
- Water district staff or operations meetings
- Planning and review sessions
- Technical working group meetings
- Local organization or community project meetings
- Follow-up meetings where action items must be tracked

## Notes for Customization

- Use factual, neutral, and concise wording.
- Record decisions and action items clearly.
- Avoid unnecessary personal remarks or side discussions.
- Attach attendance sheets, presentations, reports, or photos when needed.
- Confirm the approval process for official committees, boards, councils, or project groups.
- If the meeting is online, note the platform and method of attendance confirmation.
- Keep minutes in the office records system for future reference.

## Disclaimer

This template is for general documentation assistance only. Official bodies, councils, boards, committees, and offices should follow their required format, quorum rules, approval procedures, and records policies where applicable.

Meeting minutes should be reviewed and approved by the proper person or body before being treated as official.
