## 3. Content Strategy for Batch 2

The second batch should strengthen the site around practical public-service documentation and small-office workflows.

Focus areas:

- LGU documentation
- Barangay documentation
- Water district public communication
- Office administration
- Records and reporting
- Small-office website and digital readiness

The batch should continue the same approach as the launch content: useful, plain-language, search-friendly resources that can stand alone and also support internal linking across related templates and guides.

## 9. Risk and Disclaimer Notes

Topics that need stronger disclaimer language:

- Certificate of Indigency Template
- Barangay Resolution Template
- Customer Complaint Form Template
- Project Monitoring Report Template, if used for official reporting
- Basic Website Content Checklist for Small Offices, if cybersecurity or privacy is mentioned

Disclaimer guidance:

- Templates are for general reference and drafting assistance only.
- Users must review and adapt templates according to office policies, applicable laws, and approval procedures.
- Do not claim legal sufficiency, official approval, or compliance guarantee.
- Avoid wording that implies the template replaces review by authorized personnel, legal counsel, auditors, technical staff, or approving officers.

## 10. Publishing Approach

Use the same controlled workflow as Batch 1:

1. Create markdown drafts in the repo.
2. Review drafts for accuracy, tone, formatting, internal links, and disclaimers.
3. Import to WordPress staging first.
4. QA staging.
5. Publish to production only after approval.
6. Monitor indexing after publishing.

Publishing boundaries:

- Do not change WordPress production content until approved.
- Do not modify server configuration.
- Do not activate SheetBot links, lead capture forms, AdSense, or premium downloads.
- Do not modify SheetBot files or configuration.
